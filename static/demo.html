Organica Biotech — AI Assistant (Demo)

Internal preview for management. Live data, real product knowledge.

Try it

Click the green chat button at the bottom-right and ask anything, e.g.:

  • “My paddy crop has low yield and salinity stress — what do you recommend?”
  • “Our STP has high COD and a foul odour. Which product helps?”
  • “Ammonia is high in my shrimp pond. Any solution?”
  • “Can you share the datasheet and a quote for Cleanmaxx STP?”

Every conversation and any captured lead (name / email / location) is recorded and visible to the team in the admin dashboard.
